Credits

What our surfaces are built on.

The surfaces on this site carry SyMoNeuRaL names — SymonChat, SymonImage, SymonServe, SymonRender, RavenCalc. Underneath them is a stack of permissively licensed open-source work by other people. This page is where that work is credited, in full, in one place, with the licence each component is used under and a plain statement of what we changed.

It is deliberately one page rather than a line under every heading. The obligation these licences create is that the notice travels with what we distribute, not that it is repeated on every screen. So it is here, it is complete, and it is linked from the footer of every page and from the rack itself.

Modifications

What we changed, stated plainly

Apache-2.0 asks anyone redistributing modified files to say that they modified them. We did modify them, in two ways, and here is the statement.

Statement of modification

The model weights on this machine are not the weights their authors published. Every model we serve has been quantised from its released precision to a smaller on-disk format — GGUF Q4_K_M for the Qwen2.5 chat models, q4_k for FLUX.1-schnell and its text encoders. That is a modification of the licensed files, and it is why the models fit on one consumer card at all.

The chat model is additionally fine-tuned. SymonChat runs Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with a LoRA adapter that SymonSays LLC authored and owns. The adapter changes how the model writes and what it will and will not claim. A LoRA adapter is a derived work of its base model, and it is stated here for that reason.

Neither change is upstream's doing. If SymonChat says something wrong, that is ours, not the Qwen team's; if a render disappoints, that is our four steps and our quantisation, not Black Forest Labs'.

A note on why our names are on the surfaces at all: Apache-2.0 grants copyright and patent rights, and explicitly grants no trademark rights. Building on Qwen and FLUX is permitted; branding a product as them is not. Naming our surfaces SymonChat and SymonImage and crediting the upstream work here is the posture the licence actually points at, not a way around it.

hls.js is here because Chrome and Firefox will not play an HLS stream natively; Safari will, and the page uses the browser's own path there. It was chosen over a WebAssembly build of VLC specifically on licensing: VLC is GPLv2, and shipping a GPL blob inside these pages would place the whole site under that licence. That is the kind of decision this page exists to be honest about.

There used to be a second row here. The API reference at /docs was rendered by a third-party documentation UI — 1.6 MB of Apache-2.0 bundle served from this webroot, with its LICENSE and NOTICE beside it. It is now rendered by our own code, reading the same openapi.json the service already publishes, so that bundle and its notice files are no longer distributed and the obligation no longer applies. The row is described here instead of deleted, because an attribution that quietly disappears looks exactly like one that was stripped, and this page is worth more if it also records what it stopped shipping.
